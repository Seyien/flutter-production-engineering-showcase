## Publication boundary

- The source recordings remain outside this repository and were not modified.
- The four approved CapCut exports are published without trimming, blurring, or re-encoding.
- Every file is below GitHub's per-file size limit.
- Videos demonstrate product interaction only; they do not expose application source code, private endpoints, credentials, or database details.

Public-facing community handles/comments and third-party visual content may appear as part of the recorded product UI. Their publication in this portfolio was explicitly approved by the repository owner.

Third-party content appears only as part of the recorded application UI. All related rights remain with their respective owners.

## Reproduction helper

[`create-clips.ps1`](create-clips.ps1) is a local helper for producing new clips from future sanitized recordings. Generated output remains ignored until it passes the same privacy and publication review.
